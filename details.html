The Details

Everything you need to know.

What's included, what it costs, and where your spot would be.

At a glance

Simple, honest, country living.

Opened in April 2024, the Oasis has 4 flat-ish acres. Some spots have shore power hookups.

No septic is offered, so all tenants must be on composting toilet or use the outhouse onsite.

Services included

  • Weekly garbage collection (be reasonable about it, it's household, not industrial)
  • Fresh water supply via hose at the house (working on a well)
  • 50mbps wifi reaches to the creek
  • Outhouse
  • Creek access

Not included

  • Water hookups for camping. You're welcome to our well at the house, but we don't have water at tent sites.
  • Human waste: at this time we require composting toilets or tanks. We do have an outhouse with composting toilet.
